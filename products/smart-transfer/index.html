Smart Transfer

Money Transfer Without Borders

International and domestic peer-to-peer payments through the mobile phones and social media apps you already use.

Overview

Smart Transfer facilitates international and domestic peer-to-peer payments through mobile phones and social media apps, using digital ledger technology to move money quickly and securely between people — without borders getting in the way.

What Smart Transfer does

Peer-to-peer

Send money directly between people, person to person, without intermediaries in the middle.

International & domestic

Move money across borders or within the country from the same app.

Through apps you use

Transfer via mobile phones and the social media apps already on your phone.

Digital ledger technology

Built on digital ledger technology for secure, traceable transfers.

Mobile-first

Designed for the phone in your pocket — no branch visit required.

Social & simple

Sending money feels as easy as sending a message.

How it works

1

Connect

Open Smart Transfer through your mobile or social app.

2

Send

Choose a recipient and send money — domestic or international.

3

Settle

Digital ledger technology settles the transfer securely.
