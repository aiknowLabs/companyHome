Flyx

A peer-to-peer exchange platform that matches you with other people exchanging the currency you need.

Overview

Flyx is a unique peer-to-peer exchange platform that empowers you to be matched with other customers exchanging the other side's currency. Instead of going through a money changer, Flyx pairs you directly with someone who needs exactly what you have.

What Flyx does

Peer-to-peer matching

Get matched directly with other customers exchanging the opposite currency.

Get local currency

Need local currency on arrival? Find someone exchanging the other way.

No middle changer

Exchange directly between people instead of through a traditional money changer.

Two-sided

Both sides get the currency they want from a single match.

Simple

"Just flyx it" — request, match, and exchange.

For travellers

Made for people who need the other side's currency, wherever they are.

How it works

1

Request

Tell Flyx which currency you have and which you need.

2

3

Exchange

Complete the peer-to-peer exchange directly.
